# Contoso Ltd. — Lab Organization Design

Status: Proposed design. This document does not confirm that groups or memberships have been created.

## Company

Contoso Ltd. is a fictional company with 20 employees across IT, Sales, Marketing, and HR. This lab initially uses one test user; additional users can be added during Week 2.

## Department and Group Plan

| Department | Planned employees | Proposed security group | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|
| IT | 2 | SG-IT | Group users who require IT resource access |
| Sales | 8 | SG-Sales | Group users who require sales resource access |
| Marketing | 6 | SG-Marketing | Group users who require marketing resource access |
| HR | 4 | SG-HR | Group users who require HR resource access |

Use assigned membership for the initial lab. Group membership alone does not grant access until permissions are assigned to a resource. Membership in SG-IT does not assign an administrator role.

Microsoft 365 groups for collaboration will be designed separately when the lab requires shared collaboration resources.
